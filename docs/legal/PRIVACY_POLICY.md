## 1. Who we are

TrustLink Logistics ("TrustLink", "we", "us") provides last-mile parcel and document delivery in Abuja, Nigeria, through the TrustLink customer app, TrustLink Rider app, and admin console.

This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the **Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA)** and applicable regulations.

## 2. Data we collect

**Account and identity data**
- Phone number, first and last name, email address (optional), role (customer or rider), profile photo/avatar if you add one.
- Rider KYC: NIN, government ID front/back images, selfie, vehicle registration, vehicle type, plate number, and review status.
- Authentication data: hashed OTP records, session tokens, login timestamps, account status.

**Delivery data**
- Pickup and drop-off addresses, contact names and phone numbers of sender and recipient, package type, weight, description, fare, delivery status, timeline events, and proof of delivery (photo and recipient confirmation).
- Rider location while a delivery is active, if the rider app sends it.

**Payment data**
- Payment reference, amount, currency, provider, status, channel, and gateway response.
- We do **not** store your full card number or CVV. Card data is handled by Paystack/Flutterwave.

**Support and communications**
- Support issues, descriptions, attachments, and resolution notes.
- In-app notifications, SMS delivery status, and read state.

**Device and technical data**
- App version, device platform, IP address, request timestamps, and error/diagnostic logs.
- We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party ad tracking in R1.

## 3. Why we use it

- Create and verify your account, send OTPs, and keep you signed in.
- Create, price, dispatch, track, and complete deliveries.
- Let riders receive jobs, prove pickup/delivery, and let customers track progress.
- Process payments, prevent fraud, and handle refunds/chargebacks.
- Review rider KYC and keep the platform safe.
- Provide customer support and respond to complaints.
- Send service SMS and in-app notifications.
- Meet legal, tax, accounting, and regulatory obligations.
- Improve reliability and debug failures using aggregated or diagnostic data.

## 4. Lawful bases

We rely on one or more of: performance of a contract (providing deliveries), consent (optional marketing, if introduced), legal obligation (tax, fraud, KYC), and legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement) balanced against your rights.

## 5. Who we share data with

- **Riders** assigned to your delivery: the pickup/drop-off details, recipient name and phone, package details, and delivery instructions needed to complete the job.
- **Customers** see the assigned rider's name, vehicle type/plate, and delivery status.
- **Paystack / Flutterwave** for payment processing and verification.
- **Termii / Africa's Talking** for SMS OTP and delivery notifications.
- **Cloudflare R2 / AWS S3** for storing KYC and proof-of-delivery images.
- **Expo/EAS** for app builds and crash/diagnostic data, if enabled.
- **Hosting and database providers** that run the API and managed Postgres.
- **Professional advisers, auditors, regulators, or law enforcement** where required by law or to protect rights and safety.
- **A successor** in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy.

We do not sell personal data.

## 6. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside Nigeria. Where required, we use contractual safeguards and provider data-processing terms consistent with the NDPA.

## 7. Retention

- Account data: while your account is active, then as needed for legal/tax/audit obligations.
- OTP records: short-lived (minutes) and then purged or anonymised.
- Delivery records and receipts: for the period required for tax, accounting, dispute resolution, and fraud prevention.
- KYC records: while the rider account is active and for the legally required period afterwards.
- Proof-of-delivery images: as needed to resolve disputes and meet legal obligations.
- Support records: for a reasonable period after resolution.
- Logs: short retention unless needed for an investigation.

When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

## 8. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection to processing, and you may withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Use the contact details above. We may need to verify your identity.

You can delete your account in the app (Account → Delete account) or by contacting us. Deletion anonymises your profile and closes the account; we may retain transaction, tax, fraud, and dispute records as required by law, and we block deletion while a delivery is active.

You may complain to the **Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)** if you believe we have not handled your request properly.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS, hashed OTPs, hashed passwords for admin accounts, role-based access, rate limiting, scoped database access, and private object storage. No method is perfect; report a suspected incident to [security@yourdomain.com].

## 10. Children

The service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. Contact us if you believe a child has created an account.

## 11. Cookies and the admin console

The admin console uses browser storage for the session token and user profile. It does not use advertising cookies. The mobile apps use secure device storage for the session token.
