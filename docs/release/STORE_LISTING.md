**Category:** Business
**Keywords (Apple, ≤100):** rider jobs Abuja, delivery rider, courier work, dispatch, parcels, logistics

**Full description**

TrustLink Rider is the rider-side app for the TrustLink delivery network in Abuja.

Go online, receive dispatch jobs, review pickup and drop-off details, and move each delivery through pickup, in-transit, and proof of delivery. Capture the delivery photo and the recipient’s confirmation code, and get back online for the next job.

For riders:
- Online/offline availability
- Assigned job details and customer contact
- Step-by-step pickup and delivery flow
- Proof-of-delivery photo and recipient code
- KYC document upload from the phone
- In-app alerts and SMS updates
- Delivery history

Rider requirements: valid ID, NIN, a roadworthy vehicle, and approval after KYC review.

TrustLink Rider is for approved riders in the Abuja launch area.
