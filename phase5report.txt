### Fare Calculation Logic
- **Base Fare**: Fixed base charge
- **Distance Charge**: `distanceKm × perKmRate × distanceBandMultiplier`
- **Weight Charge**: `weightKg × perKgRate × weightTierMultiplier`
- **Subtotal**: `baseFare + distanceCharge + weightCharge`
- **Total Fare**: `max(subtotal, minimumFare)`
